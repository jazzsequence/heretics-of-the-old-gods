
# Sources

The books a character can be built from in **Heretics of the Old Gods**. If an ancestry, subclass, feat, or background comes from something on this list, it's fair game. Anything not listed needs a DM okay first.

**Baseline is the 2024 rules.** Backgrounds are how you pick culture (see [[Ancestries]]), and ancestry stat blocks are pulled from whichever book that lineage lives in. Where a book has both a 2014 and a 2024 version of an option, use the 2024 version unless told otherwise.

---

## Core Ruleset

**Player's Handbook (2024)** — The default. Every base subclass, the ancestries, feats, and the background-as-culture system all start here.

## Official D&D Expansions (Wizards of the Coast)

- **Xanathar's Guide to Everything** — extra subclasses across most classes, plus feats.
- **Tasha's Cauldron of Everything** — more subclasses across most classes.
- **Explorer's Guide to Wildemount** — the Critical Role Wildemount book. Source for the goblinoid, dragonborn-lineage, and other Wildemount ancestries, plus the Echo Knight fighter.
- **Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica** — Ravnica subclass content (e.g. druid).
- **Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft** — gothic-horror subclasses (bard, warlock).
- **Eberron: Forge of the Artificer** — the 2025 Eberron artificer book. Source for the Warforged (Aeormaton) and Changeling ancestries and the artificer specialists.

## Critical Role / Darrington Press

- **Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn** — Exandria-native subclasses across several classes.
- **Blood Hunter (2022)** — Matt Mercer's Blood Hunter class and its Orders.

## Third-Party Books

Owned/DM-provided. Not on the standard SRD, so check with the DM if you don't have access.

- **The Crooked Moon** — folk-horror subclasses spanning nearly every class.
- **Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting** — subclasses across bard, cleric, druid, ranger, sorcerer, warlock, wizard.
- **Zaman's Guide to the End of Time** — gunner archetypes and a rogue archetype.
- **Hexbound** — druid and ranger subclasses.
- **Scavenger** — druid, fighter, and rogue subclasses.

## Playtest (Unofficial)

- **Unearthed Arcana: Apocalyptic Subclasses** — druid and fighter options. Playtest material — allowed but subject to change; clear it with the DM.

## Homebrew / Campaign-Specific

Written for this setting. See the individual option pages for details.

- **Campaign homebrew** — custom feats and artificer specialists.
- **Homebrew — Heretics of the Old Gods** — cleric domains tied to Nuxan's deities, and merged wizard schools (Evocation + Necromancy, Divination + Illusion, Chronurgy + Graviturgy).


# Nicodranas

## Overview

Major port city on the [[Menagerie Coast]]. Home to [[Yussa Errenis]]'s Tidepeak tower (pre-Exodus). Part of the [[Clovis Concord]]'s network of coastal city-states. In 1000 A.E. it is a drowned, abandoned ruin — the epicenter of the Bleed, and nothing willingly sails from it.
